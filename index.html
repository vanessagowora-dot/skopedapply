01What is SKOPED?

We connect people to the services and skills they need.

SKOPED is a Zimbabwe-based ecosystem for connections and opportunities. We introduce customers to trusted independent providers, and providers to customers.

02The opportunity

Not a job. A business to build. Successful candidates become SKOPED’s founding members.

/ 01

Own a function

Take charge of an area that fits your skills: marketing, partnerships, operations or technology.

/ 02

Shape it early

Help decide how SKOPED is built, positioned and grown.

/ 03

Build with a team

Work remotely and flexibly with other founding members, each with clear responsibilities.

03Why join

Real benefits, and real revenue streams to build.

SKOPED is set up to earn in several ways. Founding members help build these streams.

01

Referral commissions

Earnings when SKOPED introduces a customer to a provider.

02

Provider listings

Providers pay to be listed in the SKOPED network.

03

Promotion

Featured placement and promotional services for providers.

  • A real stake in a business from its first days
  • The chance to become an equity-holding founding member
  • Flexible, remote-first work
  • Room to grow as new divisions open

SKOPED is in its early stages. Nothing is guaranteed. Earnings, ownership, contributions and roles are discussed and agreed in writing before anyone commits. There is no fee to apply.
